# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary users are logistics fleet buyers, enterprise transport managers, construction-material carriers, cold-chain operators, urban delivery operators, and business owners comparing commercial truck options. They arrive with a procurement job to do: understand which truck configuration fits their route, load, delivery timeline, financing needs, and after-sales expectations, then move toward a quote or advisor conversation.

## Product Purpose

This site presents a high-end commercial truck sales showroom for heavy-duty transport scenarios. It should make the truck lineup feel credible, configurable, and procurement-ready, while converting qualified visitors into quote, consultation, and model-configuration leads. Success means the page communicates capability quickly, shows real-feeling vehicle options, and gives buyers a clear path from product interest to commercial inquiry.

## Brand Personality

Professional, rugged, trustworthy. The voice is direct and sales-oriented without cheap urgency. The experience should feel like an industrial equipment showroom for serious fleet decisions: controlled, precise, durable, and commercially competent.

## Anti-references

Do not make the site feel like cyberpunk, purple-blue gradient SaaS, glassmorphism, neon-heavy visuals, cheap marketplace ecommerce, cartoon illustration, generic three-column SaaS feature blocks, cluttered parameter dumps, low-quality stock imagery, or a luxury sports-car brand site. Avoid over-polished lifestyle drama when the buyer needs credible commercial equipment detail.

## Design Principles

Show the truck first. The vehicle is the main proof point, so page hierarchy should put 3/4 perspective truck imagery, configuration data, and direct inquiry actions ahead of decorative messaging.

Make procurement feel controlled. Navigation, model comparison, specs, language selection, and CTA flows should reduce uncertainty for a business buyer evaluating fleet options.

Use industrial restraint. Visual energy should come from scale, perspective, metal-like structure, typography, and disciplined orange accents rather than spectacle or trend effects.

Keep every claim operational. Copy should connect to load, route, delivery, body build, warranty, financing, and service support instead of vague slogans.

Support international buyers. Multilingual content and language selection should be first-class, compact, and reliable without overwhelming the header.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Default target is WCAG AA for contrast and interaction clarity. Support reduced motion preferences, keep text readable over light industrial backgrounds, avoid relying on color alone for state, and preserve keyboard-accessible controls for navigation, language selection, carousel actions, and CTAs.
